    Per-variant models — maintain a separate GP (pset, training) per VRP variant. => potentially better specialized performance, slightly more management overhead.

Start: Create a list of all unvisited customers.
Main Loop (Repeat until all customers are visited):
    Get a New Truck: Start a new empty route at the Depot.
    Route Building Loop (While truck can still work):

        Identify Candidates: Look at all unvisited customers.
            Filter: Ignore customers who are too heavy for the current truck.
            Filter: Ignore customers who are too far for the current battery.

        Check for End of Route:
            If no customers fit, or if the battery is low, drive the truck back to the Depot (recharging on the way if needed).
            Stop this route and go back to step 2 (get a new truck).

        Score Candidates:
            For every valid customer, calculate a Score using the input Scoring Rule.
            Example: If the rule is "Minimize Distance", the closest customer gets the best score.

        Select Best Customer:
            Pick the customer with the best score.

        Execute Move:
            Drive to that customer.
            (If the battery is too low to reach them directly, insert a visit to a Charging Station first).
            Add the customer to the route.
            Update the truck's remaining load and battery.
            Mark the customer as "Visited."

Finish: Return the list of all completed routes.
